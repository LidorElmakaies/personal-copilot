Panel surface

Ported from example/frontend's GlassPanel.tsx, not designed here — a gradient ring border (accent color fading to transparent, via a 1px-padded background gradient) plus a soft accent glow, deliberately with no backdrop blur. The current panel (GlowCard.js) uses a flat solid border + a real blur, which smears whatever's behind it (now-colorful stars/meteors) into a soft wash instead of keeping them as sharp dots.
