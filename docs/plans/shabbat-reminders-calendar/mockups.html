Turn on notifications?

Get your candle-lighting reminder on this phone, even when the app is closed.

Turn onNot now

After login you land on Home; this sheet opens over it. "Turn on" is the tap the browser needs before it shows its own Allow / Block prompt.

  • Shown only when this browser isn't subscribed, notifications aren't blocked, and you haven't picked "Not now" on this browser before
  • "Not now" is remembered per browser; the Account card (N2) is the way back
  • Already allowed and subscribed? Nothing is shown; the subscription is quietly re-sent to the server

A card between Theme and Account, same Row + Switch as the Theme card. The switch subscribes or unsubscribes this browser only, not your other devices.

Mix and match

C2's agenda and C1's grid can be one screen with a List / Month switch at the top. H1 or H2 fit any calendar choice.

Where the times come from

Candle lighting, Havdalah and holidays are calculated from your coordinates and date. A small backend service behind Gateway (or on-device with @hebcal/core) can do this.

Reminders later

Every design leaves a slot for reminders: the day card (C1), inline amber items (C2), or a per-day list (C3). Push delivery comes with that feature.
